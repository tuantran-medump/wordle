Letterlock

How to play

Guess the hidden English word. Pick a length from 5 to 8 letters; you get one more guess than the word has letters.

After each guess the tiles change colour:

  • A right letter, right spot
  • B in the word, wrong spot
  • C not in the word

Daily gives everyone the same word each day for each length. Free play gives a new random word whenever you like.
